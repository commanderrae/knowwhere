This Day in History - April 17th

  • 1917 — Second Battle of Gaza Begins As the major Allied offensive masterminded by Robert Nivelle was failing miserably on the Western Front, British forces in Palestine make their second attempt to capture the city of Gaza from the Ottoman army on April 17, 1917.
  • 1941 - Yugoslavia Surrenders to the Nazis During World War II, representatives of Yugoslavia’s various regions sign an armistice with Nazi Germany at Belgrade, ending 11 days of futile resistance against the invading German Wehrmacht. More than 300,000 Yugoslav officers and soldiers were taken prisoner. Only 200 Germans died in the conquest of Yugoslavia.
  • 1960 - Bay of Pigs Invasion Begins The Bay of Pigs invasion begins when a CIA-financed and -trained group of Cuban refugees lands in Cuba and attempts to topple the communist government of Fidel Castro. The attack was an utter failure.
  • 1964 — Jerrie Mock becomes the first woman to complete a solo flight around the world, landing her single-engine Cessna 180 — nicknamed Spirit of Columbus — back in Columbus, Ohio after a 29-day journey covering 23,000 miles.
  • 1970 — The crew of Apollo 13 safely splashes down in the Pacific Ocean, four days after an oxygen tank explosion crippled the spacecraft en route to the Moon. NASA's improvised rescue effort is widely regarded as one of the greatest feats of engineering problem-solving in history.
  • 1975 — The Khmer Rouge under Pol Pot seizes Phnom Penh, beginning one of the most brutal regimes of the 20th century. The subsequent genocide would claim the lives of an estimated 1.5 to 2 million people — nearly a quarter of Cambodia's population.
  • 1989 - Chinese Students Protest Against Government Thousands of Chinese students take to the streets in Beijing to protest government policies and issue a call for greater democracy in the communist People’s Republic of China (PRC). The protests grew until the Chinese government ruthlessly suppressed them in June during what came to be known as the Tiananmen Square Massacre.
  • 2007 — The Virginia Tech massacre occurs when a student opens fire across campus in Blacksburg, Virginia, killing 32 people and injuring 17 others. It remains the deadliest mass shooting by a single gunman in U.S. history.
